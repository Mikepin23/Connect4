Connect 4

How to play:

  • Click on any column or use the keyboard to play:
    • Use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move your disc.
    • Press Spacebar to place your disc.
  • The disc will drop down to the lowest available space in the chosen column.
  • The game ends when one player successfully connects four of their coloured discs in a row, either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
